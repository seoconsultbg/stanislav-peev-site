AI search is where your next clients already are

A growing share of buying decisions now starts as a question to ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews - and those answers cite only a handful of sources. Being one of them is the new page one. That's what generative engine optimization (GEO) does, and it's been my primary research focus since the field emerged.

Most providers are retrofitting "AI" onto old SEO playbooks. I've been running the experiments. Since 2023 I've measured how AI engines select sources, what makes a passage citable, and how brand mentions substitute for links in AI answers - and published the results, including a proposed metric (Reference Rate) for tracking AI citations the way we once tracked CTR.

The practical work behind a GEO engagement: entity and Knowledge Graph building so AI systems know who you are; passage-level restructuring so your pages are quotable; brand-mention campaigns; llms.txt and AI-crawler configuration; and citation tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot and AI Overviews.

And because AI engines lean on the same fundamentals Google rewards - authority, structure, genuinely useful content - GEO done right makes your classic SEO stronger, not separate.

Frequently asked questions

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of making your brand visible and citable in AI-generated answers - Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot. It builds on classic SEO (entities, structured data, authority) and adds passage-level citability, brand-mention signals and AI-crawler accessibility. I've been publishing original GEO research since the field emerged.

Why does AI search visibility matter now?

A growing share of buying decisions starts as a question to ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews instead of a classic search. Those answers cite only a handful of sources - being one of them is the new page one. Because so few businesses optimize for AI answers yet, early movers gain visibility much faster than in classic SEO.

What does an SEO consultant actually do?

An SEO consultant diagnoses why a website underperforms in search and builds a plan to fix it: technical health (crawling, indexing, Core Web Vitals), content that matches search intent, entity and authority signals, and measurement. Unlike an agency retainer, you work directly with the specialist who does the analysis - no account managers in between.
