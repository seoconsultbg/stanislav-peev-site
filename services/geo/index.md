## Ranking in Google is not the same as being cited by AI

In October 2026 I took 407 Google queries that had shown an AI Overview a week earlier and compared every page the overviews cited with the organic results for the same query, down to position 100. **65% of the cited pages did not rank in the top 100 at all.** ChatGPT went further: 91% of the pages it cited were outside Google's top 100 for the query it was asked.

## What AI SEO consulting covers

### A measured AI visibility baseline

A fixed set of buyer prompts, the questions your customers actually ask, run several times on each engine, because answers change from run to run. In my [60-query tracking test](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/ai-visibility-tracking-reliability/), the choice of engine moved a brand's measured visibility about 2.3 times more than the model's own randomness. So the baseline is per engine, never one blended score.

### A map of the sources AI reads

Which pages each engine cites for your category: listicles, comparison pages, review platforms, forums, your own pages. The shape of the question decides a lot. In my [pilot](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/ai-citations-by-query-shape/), "best X" questions cited listicles 84% of the time, while "X alternatives" questions cited comparison pages 86% of the time.

### Wrong facts, fixed at the source

Outdated hours, wrong prices, services you stopped offering, a brand name confused with someone else's. I trace each error to the page the model read and fix it there, on your site and on third-party profiles. [93% of businesses](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/ai-wrong-business-information-statistics/) have at least one wrong or missing fact in AI answers.

### Pages AI can quote accurately

Pages that answer one buyer question clearly in the first lines, with the specifics a model needs: what you do, for whom, where, at what terms, compared with what. Structured data that matches the visible text. Written for people first, so a model can quote them without guessing.

### Presence in the sources AI already trusts

Getting into the lists, comparisons and directories the engines cite for your category, on merit. Not through self-ranked "best of" pages: in one 100-query test, AI Overviews cited such lists 323 times and still didn't recommend their author in 69% of cases. [The evidence is here.](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/owned-review-sites-ai-visibility/)

### Technical access for AI crawlers

Whether OpenAI, Perplexity, Anthropic and Google crawlers can reach your pages, which robots.txt and CDN rules block them, whether content renders without JavaScript, and how bot settings on your host behave. I've seen shared-hosting firewalls return 403 errors to AI crawlers without the owner ever knowing.

Mine does. There is no secret trick for AI answers. The extra work sits on top of a site that already works.

## AI SEO consulting questions, answered

**What does an AI SEO consultant do?**

Works on how AI assistants answer questions about your market: whether ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot and AI Overviews name you, which pages they cite, and whether the facts are right. In practice that's a measured baseline, a source map, fixes at the source, citable pages, third-party presence, crawler access and repeat measurement.

**Is AI SEO consulting better than a traditional SEO agency?**

They're not substitutes. AI features rest on the same ranking systems, so weak fundamentals limit AI visibility too. The difference is the extra layer: 65% of AI Overview citations in my data didn't rank in the top 100 for the query, so sources, facts and measurement need their own work.

**Is generative engine optimization worth the investment?**

If your buyers ask AI before they buy, usually yes - but measure it properly. Analytics undercount AI: CallRail tracked 0.073% of small-business calls to AI, while companies that asked customers saw far more (Outfunnel: 7.29% of leads named ChatGPT or AI). I combine prompt tracking, a "how did you hear about us" field and branded search. More in my [attribution study](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/ai-lead-attribution-gap/).

**Can you guarantee ChatGPT will recommend my business?**

No, and nobody can. Answers change between runs and engines; the median AI Overview in my data replaced half its cited pages within a week. What I commit to is a baseline, specific fixes and re-measurement on the same prompts.

**How long does it take to show up in AI answers?**

Fixing wrong facts can move faster than rankings, because AI search keeps re-reading its sources. Being recommended in a competitive category takes longer and depends on the third-party sources engines trust. The baseline gives you a realistic estimate for your market first.

**Do I need an llms.txt file?**

It's optional. Google Search doesn't use it, so it's never the main lever. I add one when it's cheap to maintain, after crawler access, clear pages, consistent facts and third-party sources.

**Do you work with e-commerce, local and multilingual sites?**

Yes. The method stays the same, the sources differ: product and comparison pages for stores, Google Business Profile and review platforms for local businesses, per-language prompt sets for multilingual sites.

**How much does AI SEO consulting cost?**

Scoped over email and priced fixed up front: a one-time AI visibility baseline, or a monthly GEO program in one of three depths. Scope depends on markets, languages and engines. No hourly billing, no long lock-in.
