Service questions, answered

What does a GEO program include?

An AI-visibility baseline and ongoing citation tracking across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot and Google AI Overviews; passage-level content restructuring for citability; entity and Knowledge Graph building; brand-mention campaigns; and llms.txt plus AI-crawler configuration. The methodology is backed by my published GEO research.

What do your SEO consultant services include?

Monthly SEO consulting covers technical SEO monitoring, content strategy and briefs, entity and authority building, structured data, and reporting against agreed KPIs. It pairs naturally with a GEO program, since AI engines reward the same fundamentals Google does.

How do you price engagements?

Every engagement is scoped individually after a short email exchange, because two sites of the same size rarely need the same work. You get a fixed audit price or a fixed monthly fee up front - no hourly billing, no long-term lock-in. If it turns out your project needs less, I'll recommend the smaller option.

How long until results show?

Technical fixes often move metrics within weeks. Competitive rankings typically take 3-6 months of consistent work. AI-citation visibility can move faster because fewer competitors optimize for it - which is exactly why early GEO investment pays off. I set expected timelines per task in the roadmap - and I don't sell guarantees no honest SEO can make.

Can you train my in-house team instead?

Yes - the personalized 15-hour online SEO course is built around your actual website, so your team learns on the project they'll maintain.
