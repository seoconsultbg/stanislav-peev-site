# Small business SEO without the agency retainer

Small businesses don't need less SEO expertise - they need it in smaller, honest doses. I'll tell you what to do yourself for free, what's worth paying for, and what to skip. **Senior work, scoped to a small business budget, no lock-in.**

## Why small business SEO is a different job

Most SEO services are shaped for companies with marketing teams and five-figure monthly budgets. Sold to a small business, the same package turns into junior staff executing a generic template - which is why so many owners describe SEO vendors with one word: snake oil. The economics are the problem, not the discipline.

For a small business the leverage is different. You don't need 40 deliverables a month; you need the **right five things done in the right order** - and most of them are cheap or free. You also face a threat bigger companies feel less: AI assistants get small businesses wrong far more often. In the largest accuracy study to date, [50% of small businesses received at least one fabricated fact in AI answers, versus 32% of large companies](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/ai-wrong-business-information-statistics/) - and AI confuses small business brand names five times more often. With 45% of consumers now asking AI for recommendations, that's not a curiosity; it's a leak.

## Do these yourself first - they're free

I'd rather you spend nothing before you know what's worth spending on. This list puts you ahead of most local competitors:

- **Complete your Google Business Profile** - every field, real photos, and a steady flow of reviews. For local businesses this single asset outweighs most website work. Bing Places too: it feeds Copilot and DuckDuckGo.
- **Set up Google Search Console** - it's free and shows exactly which searches you appear in and where your pages fail.
- **Fix titles and descriptions** - name the service and the place; your customers search "emergency plumber austin", not your slogan.
- **Write pages in your customers' words** - listen to how people describe the problem on the phone, and use those exact phrases. One page per service, one per location you serve.
- **Keep name, address and phone identical everywhere** - your site, GBP, Yelp, Foursquare, directories. Inconsistency confuses both Google and AI assistants, and AI reads those third-party sources more than it reads you.
- **Ask AI about your business** - fresh chats in ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity: hours, phone, services, "best [your category] in [city]". Wrong answers point to the sources you need to fix. My [10-prompt self-audit](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/ai-wrong-business-information-statistics/) walks you through it.

Owners who've gone the DIY road estimate it takes them 3-10x longer than working with a professional - but for the basics above, that trade is usually worth it. Where it stops being worth it is diagnosis and strategy: knowing *why* traffic isn't coming and what to do next.

## Small business SEO questions, answered

**Is SEO worth it for a small business?**

Usually - but not always as a monthly retainer. Do the free basics, buy an audit to learn what your situation is worth, then decide on ongoing spend. Sometimes the honest answer is "not yet".

**How much does small business SEO cost?**

Scoped individually, priced fixed up front - audit, light consulting or training. No hourly billing, no contracts, no lock-in. The scoping conversation is free and happens over email.

**What can I do myself for free?**

Complete your Google Business Profile and Bing Places, gather reviews, fix titles and descriptions, set up Search Console, keep name-address-phone consistent everywhere, and write service pages in your customers' words. The checklist above covers each step.

**Do small businesses need to care about AI search?**

More than anyone: 50% of small businesses get at least one fabricated fact in AI answers (vs 32% of large companies), and AI confuses small brand names 5x more often - while 45% of consumers now ask AI for recommendations. Checking and fixing what AI says about you is basic hygiene now.

**Agency or consultant for a small business?**

Senior time in small doses usually beats junior time in bulk. Most retainers are built for bigger budgets; small accounts get the template. An audit plus light consulting or training fits small business economics better - scale up when the numbers justify it.

**How long until results?**

Local improvements often show within weeks; competitive rankings take 3-6 months of consistent work; AI answer accuracy can move faster. Thirty-day page-one promises are a red flag, not a plan.
