Where the clicks actually are

Most of the work in classic search engine optimization is moving pages that already rank into the positions people click in the search results. What monthly SEO consulting covers

Strategy and priorities

An SEO strategy read from your Search Console data rather than a generic keyword list: which queries and pages are worth the effort, and what comes first. Every month starts with what moved, what broke and what to do next.

Technical SEO

Crawling and indexing, redirects and canonicals, Core Web Vitals, JavaScript rendering, structured data, migrations and international setups with hreflang. I do the analysis personally and write specifications your developer can implement directly.

Content and search intent

Which page should answer which query, where pages compete with each other, what to refresh, merge or retire, and briefs for new pages built from what actually ranks for your topics.

On-page and internal linking

Titles, headings and page structure checked against the results for your queries, and internal links that send weight to the pages that earn money instead of the ones that happen to be in the menu.

Local SEO

For businesses with locations: Google Business Profile, location and service pages, reviews and consistent business details across the directories that Google and AI assistants read.

Reporting on outcomes

A monthly written report that leads with leads, calls or sales, then shows visibility, rankings and organic traffic as the supporting detail, with a log of every change made to the site.

How SEO consulting works

  1. You write to me. Your site, your markets, what you sell and what search should do for you. A few sentences is enough; the first SEO consultation happens over email.
  2. Diagnosis first. Either a full SEO audit or a lighter onboarding review with read-only access to Search Console and analytics. You get the findings in writing before any monthly commitment.
  3. The monthly cycle. Priorities for the month, technical analysis and specifications, content briefs, review of what your team ships, and a written report at the end of the month.
  4. No lock-in. A fixed monthly fee, scoped over email, with no long contract. If the numbers stop justifying it, you stop.

Everything runs over email; no calls or meetings are needed. Technical fixes can move numbers within weeks, while competitive rankings usually take 3-6 months to build momentum.

SEO consulting questions, answered

What does an SEO consultant do month to month?

Starts from your data: what moved in Search Console and analytics, which pages matter, what broke. Then sets priorities, does the technical analysis, writes specifications and briefs, reviews what your team ships and reports on outcomes - leads and sales first.

Should I hire an SEO consultant or an SEO agency?

An agency for content and links at volume on a larger budget; an independent consultant for diagnosis, priorities and oversight when you or your contractors can implement. Startups and small teams usually get more from the consultant.

How long does SEO consulting take to show results?

Technical fixes can move numbers within weeks; competitive rankings usually need 3-6 months to build momentum, longer in hard niches. Page one in 30 days is a red flag.

Do you do technical SEO yourself or only advise?

I do the technical analysis personally and write specifications your developer can implement directly. Where access and scope allow, I implement changes myself.

Do you work with international and multilingual sites?

Yes - clients in the USA, the UK, Belgium, Switzerland, Australia and Bulgaria. Hreflang, per-market content and Search Console per market are a regular part of the work.

How much does SEO consulting cost?

Scoped over email and priced as a fixed monthly fee, depending on site size, markets and how much implementation you need from me. No hourly billing, no long lock-in.
