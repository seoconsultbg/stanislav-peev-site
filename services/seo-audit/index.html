An SEO audit that tells you what to fix, in what order

A complete diagnosis of your site's search performance - technical health, content, entities and AI search visibility - condensed into a prioritized written roadmap. Performed personally, priced fixed, yours to keep whoever implements it.

What the audit diagnoses

Technical crawl & indexation

Full crawl of the site plus Search Console forensics: what Google can't reach, won't index, or indexes wrongly - redirects, canonicals, duplicates, orphan pages, crawl waste.

Core Web Vitals & rendering

Field data, not just lab scores - and a JavaScript rendering check: whether search engines and AI crawlers actually see the content your visitors see.

Content & search intent

Which pages target which queries, where intent is mismatched, where pages cannibalize each other, and which content is dead weight versus one rewrite away from earning.

On-page elements

Titles, headings, internal linking and page structure - reviewed against what actually ranks for your queries, not against a generic best-practice checklist.

Entities & structured data

How well search engines understand who you are: schema validity and gaps, entity consistency across the web, Knowledge Graph presence.

AI search visibility (GEO)

A baseline of what ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews say and cite about you - where they're wrong, which sources they read, and how citable your key pages are. Standard in every audit, because 93% of businesses have at least one fact wrong in AI answers.

What you get, and what you don't

You get a written report with a prioritized roadmap: every finding marked by expected impact and implementation effort, so your developer or team can start at the top and work down. Quick wins are separated from strategic work, and each recommendation says why - the reasoning, not just the instruction. Follow-up questions about the report are included; there's no meter running.

You don't get a 90-page export of tool warnings, a document written to scare you into a retainer, or "critical errors" copied from a crawler that don't cost you a single visit. If something in your setup is fine, the report says it's fine. If SEO isn't your best next investment, the report says that too - it has, more than once.

The process is simple: you write a few sentences about your business, I ask what I need to scope it, you get a fixed price and timeline, and the work runs over email with read-only access to Search Console and analytics. No calls, no meetings. When it makes sense to continue, the audit converts naturally into monthly consulting or a GEO program - but it's built to stand alone.

When a one-time audit is the right call

  • Traffic dropped, or never took off - and you want to know exactly why before spending another dollar on content or ads.
  • You're planning a redesign or migration - the cheapest time to find SEO problems is before they ship.
  • An agency is pitching you a retainer - an independent audit is inexpensive insurance against buying deliverables you don't need.
  • You have a team to implement - you need senior diagnosis and priorities, not another pair of hands.
  • AI assistants describe your business wrongly - and you want to know which sources to fix first.

SEO audit questions, answered

What does the SEO audit include?

Six diagnosis areas: technical crawl and indexation, Core Web Vitals and rendering, content and search-intent mapping, on-page elements, entity and structured data review, and an AI search visibility baseline. Delivered as a written report with a prioritized impact/effort roadmap.

How is this different from a free automated audit?

Tools export warnings; they can't tell you which of 400 flagged items actually costs you traffic, or that your real problem is intent mismatch or a missing entity. Here the tools are input - the deliverable is judgment: what to fix, in what order, what to ignore.

Does it cover AI search visibility?

Yes, as a standard section: what AI assistants say and cite about you, where they're wrong, and how citable your pages are. In 2026 an audit without this is incomplete - 45% of consumers already ask AI for recommendations.

What do you need from me?

Read-only access to Google Search Console and analytics, plus a few sentences about your goals and markets. Everything runs over email.

How much does it cost?

Fixed price, quoted up front after a short email exchange - scope depends on site size and markets. No hourly billing. If your site needs less than a full audit, I'll recommend the smaller option.

Will you help implement it afterwards?

The roadmap is written so your team can implement without me, and follow-up questions are always included. If you want ongoing help, it converts into monthly consulting or a GEO program.
