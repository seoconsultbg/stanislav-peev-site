Data report · AI Search

## [AI citations by question shape: which pages ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity cite for "best X", "X alternatives" and "X vs Y"](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/ai-citations-by-query-shape/)

Original pilot: 176 citations logged and every cited page labeled by shape and owner. "best X" questions cited listicles 84% of the time and comparison pages 2%; "X alternatives" flipped to 86% comparison pages, a quarter of them on competing vendors' own domains, while G2 earned one citation. Why the published "under 3%" figure for comparison pages is an average that hides the switch. Raw data published.

Data report · AI Search

## [AI referral traffic vs organic search: how it converts for a small business](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/ai-referral-traffic-conversion-vs-organic/)

Aggregated, source-verified 2026 data: AI-referred visitors convert about 50% higher than organic on Shopify product pages and are worth 4.4x per visitor (Semrush), with B2B lead conversion of 14.2% vs 2.8% for Google organic (Opollo). The catch the hype skips - volume is still tiny, and AI converted 43% worse than other channels as recently as July 2024. What it means for a small business, with limitations named.

Published August 31, 2026 · 9 min read

Data report · Local Search

## [Google review concentration: who really owns the reviews in a local market](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/google-review-concentration-home-services/)

Original dataset: 7,072 Google Business Profile listings across 6 home service trades in 12 US cities. The most-reviewed company held a top-3 Maps position in just 5.6% of 72 markets, the top 5 listings hold a median 58.3% of visible review volume, and trade predicts difficulty far better than city size. Full method, limitations and raw data published.

Published August 24, 2026 · 9 min read

## [How reliable is AI visibility tracking? A 60-query experiment](https://stanislav-peev.com/research/ai-visibility-tracking-reliability/)

I ran the same four buyer questions 60 times across ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. Which engine you track moved a brand's measured visibility 2.3x more than the model's own randomness - and two engines recommended a tool the third never named once. Original data, full method and limitations, raw dataset published.
